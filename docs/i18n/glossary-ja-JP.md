## Names kept as-is
Dave, Seraphine, Gary, Petra, Malphas, Melodia, Pemberton (ペンバートン係官), Ferro (フェロさん), Nadia, Bev, Grax, Vassago, Lilith, Lucien, Krampus, Brynhildr, Sigrún, Ottar, Hjalti, Ó Broin. Titles and roles are translated; given names stay in Latin script (honorific suffix attached directly, e.g. 大天使Bev).

## Style
Friendly です/ます for UI and onboarding; free, short, dry style for jokes and card flavor lines; numerals as Kanji in achievement titles (一万人) and Arabic in numbers with units.
