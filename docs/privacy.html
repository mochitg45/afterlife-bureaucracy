Privacy Policy — Afterlife Bureaucracy

Effective 15 September 2026. Applies to the Android app com.afterlifebureaucracy.game.

Afterlife Bureaucracy is a single-player idle game. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask you for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and the game does not send your saved progress anywhere. This policy explains the small amount of data that the third-party services inside the app do handle.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your save file — departments, staff, cards, currencies, statistics and settings — is written to your device's private app storage and stays there. We operate no server and have no database of players. Uninstalling the app deletes the save.

The export code in Settings is generated on your device and shown only to you. It is not transmitted to us. If you send it to yourself, you are the one sending it.

What third-party services collect

The app embeds three Google and RevenueCat components. They act as independent data controllers or processors under their own policies, which are linked below.

RevenueCat (with Google Play Billing) Runs the in-app purchases and the Union Membership subscription, and restores them if you reinstall.   • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy

Advertising choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, which stops it being used to personalise advertising. Google's ad settings are at adssettings.google.com. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent dialog is shown before the first ad request; if consent is declined or cannot be gathered, only non-personalised ads are served. Buying Exempt From Advertising grants a permanent ×2 on the Overnight Backlog Report. It does not change what ads the game shows, because the game never shows one you did not ask for: the rewarded buttons are optional whether or not you buy it.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to produce them. You are asked before any are scheduled and can turn them off in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of the services above, contact us and we will help you raise it with that provider.

Purchases and randomised items

The game sells Overtime Vouchers, which can be spent on the Personnel Requisition Lottery — a randomised staff-card draw. The probability of each rarity is published inside the app on the Odds screen and repeated in the store listing. Purchases are handled by Google Play; refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Your rights and how to exercise them

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. To reach the data the services above hold:

  • AdMob: reset your Advertising ID, or use Google's privacy controls.
  • RevenueCat: write to us with your purchase order ID and we will request deletion of the associated records on your behalf.
  • Play Games: delete the game's Play Games data from your Google account at myaccount.google.com.
