# Closed test brief (send with the opt-in link)

Subject: Afterlife Bureaucracy closed test

Thanks for testing Afterlife Bureaucracy, an idle clicker where you run the afterlife's
paperwork office.

How to install
1. Open the opt-in link on your Android phone and accept.
2. Install from Google Play. Updates arrive through Play like any app.

What to try (10 minutes is plenty)
- Play the intro, then stamp and hire your first clerk. Did the tutorial make sense?
- Let it run, close the app for an hour, come back. Did the Backlog Report look right?
- Sign in with Google on the title screen. Reinstall later and check your desk comes back.
- Personnel tab: pull a card with the free daily pull, open a card, equip it.
- Ledger: file an Audit when it lets you.
- Settings: switch Light / Dark theme.
- Watch one rewarded ad. Did it pay out?

Known for this round
- The Store shows "the requisition desk is closed" until purchases are set up.
- If a Play Games popup appears at launch, that is normal.

What to report
Anything that felt slow, confusing, or broken, plus your phone model and Android version.
