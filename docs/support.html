Common questions

I bought vouchers but they did not arrive. Open the Store tab and tap Restore purchases. If they are still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I cancel Union Membership? On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Afterlife Bureaucracy. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Does the game earn while it is closed? Yes. Your staff keep stamping offline, up to your offline cap, and the Overnight Backlog Report shows what they filed.

What are the card odds? Every requisition and exchange chance is listed in the game under Personnel → See odds.
